## 3. Untranslated Terms & Notation Rules
- **Brand Name**: "Glovotools" remains unchanged across all languages.
- **Mathematical Symbols**: Standard math operators (`+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `=`, `%`) and LaTeX formulas remain invariant.
- **Variable Symbols**: Latin and Greek variable symbols ($n, r, PV, FV, \mu, \sigma$) remain invariant.
- **Portuguese Rule**: Always use Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`) phrasing (e.g. *Juros*, *Prestação*, *Imóvel*) and never European Portuguese (*Juros*, *Prestações*, *Prédio*).
