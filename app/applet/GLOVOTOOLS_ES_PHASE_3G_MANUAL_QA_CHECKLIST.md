# Spanish Browser Verification Checklist

## 1. Executive Summary
This document establishes the comprehensive manual browser verification checklist for GLOVOTOOLS under the Spanish (`es`) locale. In accordance with strict Phase 3G instructions, all test scenarios are pre-configured as **NOT TESTED** pending manual browser execution by the QA engineer.

## 2. Checklist Scope & Verification Areas
1. **Spanish Homepage & Navigation**: Verify `/es/` route rendering, header links, footer, and navigation labels.
2. **Catalog & Search**: Verify All Tools catalog titles, descriptions, and search filtering in Spanish.
3. **Category & Subcategory Pages**: Verify category landing pages and subcategory headers.
4. **Calculator Workspaces**: Verify input labels, default values, validation feedback, calculation execution, and formatted results.
5. **Language Switching**: Verify switching between English (`/en/`) and Spanish (`/es/`) without session loss.
6. **Specialized Modules**: Verify PDF E-Signing (`/pdf/...`) and Document Converter interfaces.
7. **SEO & Routing**: Verify `/sitemap.xml`, canonical URLs, and hreflang alternate annotations.
8. **Responsive Layouts**: Verify desktop and mobile viewport rendering, text wrapping, and overflow resilience.
9. **Targeted Calculator Title Audit**: Verify specific tools on the Spanish homepage:
   - Merge PDF Files
   - Secure Password & Passphrase Generator
   - JSON Syntax & Structure Validator
   - Group Restaurant Bill & Tax Split Calculator

## 3. Status Classification
All checklist items initialized as **NOT TESTED**.
